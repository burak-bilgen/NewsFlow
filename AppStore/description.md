## Description

**NewsApto turns your iPhone into a smart news terminal.**

Aggregate, score, and read news from 6 world-class sources — all in one place. No ads. No tracking. No algorithms designed to keep you hooked. Just intelligent, personalized news.

### Features

**6 SOURCES, ONE FEED**
NewsApto pulls from NewsAPI, The Guardian, The New York Times, GNews, NewsData.io, and HackerNews. Every article is scored, categorized, deduplicated, and ranked automatically.

**SMART SCORING**
Our proprietary algorithm ranks articles by recency, content quality, source authority, and trending signals. The best stories rise to the top — not the loudest.

**SMART CATEGORIES**
7 categories (Technology, Business, Science, Health, Sports, Entertainment, All) with automatic detection. Powered by 150+ API mappings and 300+ keyword rules.

**TERMINAL AESTHETIC**
Matrix code rain, glitch reveal animations, neon accents — a cyberpunk reading experience hand-built in SwiftUI.

**CROSS-SOURCE DEDUP**
The same story from 3 different sources? deduplication engine keeps your feed clean and diverse.

**OFFLINE FIRST**
Two-tier cache (memory + disk) means articles you've loaded are available even without internet.

**READING LIST**
Save articles to read later, even offline. Core Data-backed persistence.

**LOCALIZED**
Full English and Turkish support. More languages coming.

### Privacy First

- Zero third-party SDKs
- No analytics, no trackers
- No account required
- All data stays on your device

### Sources

NewsApto is an independent aggregator. All article content belongs to original publishers. The app uses free tiers of each API:

- NewsAPI (250 requests/day)
- The Guardian Open Platform (500 requests/day)
- New York Times Article Search (5 requests/day)
- GNews (100 requests/day)
- NewsData.io (200 requests/day)
- HackerNews (unlimited, public API)
