# NewsFlow — App Store Description

## App Name
NewsFlow — AI-Powered News Reader

## Subtitle (iOS 15+)
Your news, intelligently sorted.

## Promotional Text (Optional)
Never miss what matters. AI-powered summaries, text-to-speech, and smart digests.

## Description

**NewsFlow is the news reader that learns what matters to you.**

Tired of endless scrolling? NewsFlow aggregates articles from top sources — NewsAPI, The Guardian, and The New York Times — then uses intelligent scoring to surface the most important stories first.

### AI-Powered Features

- **Smart Feed** — Articles are scored by recency, content quality, and relevance. The best stories rise to the top.
- **AI Summaries** — Get the key points of any article in seconds with AI-generated summaries (iOS 18+).
- **Text-to-Speech** — Listen to articles on the go. Perfect for commutes, workouts, and multitasking.
- **Breaking News Digests** — Get notified when important news breaks. Choose your frequency: once, twice, or three times daily.

### All Features

- **Multi-Source Aggregation** — NewsAPI, The Guardian, and The New York Times in one feed.
- **Smart Search** — Search across all sources with instant results.
- **Reading List** — Save articles to read later with iCloud sync support.
- **Read/Unread Tracking** — Blue dot indicators show unread articles at a glance.
- **Reading Time** — Estimated reading time for every article.
- **Share Cards** — Share beautiful article cards with friends.
- **Hero Carousel** — Top stories in an auto-scrolling carousel.
- **Browse by Source** — Explore articles from specific sources.
- **Dark Mode** — Beautiful light and dark themes.
- **Multilingual** — Full English and Turkish support.
- **Accessibility** — VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and Reduce Motion support.

### Privacy First

- No tracking, no analytics, no personal data collection.
- API keys are stored securely in the Keychain.
- All preferences stored locally on your device.
